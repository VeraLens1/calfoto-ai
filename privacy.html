Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026

This Policy explains what personal data VeraLens (“we”) processes when you use CalFoto AI, why we use it, and your rights. It is designed for GDPR (EU/EEA) and App Store / Google Play requirements.

1. Controller

The data controller is VeraLens, for the CalFoto AI app. Contact: contact.veralens@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email, Apple or Google identifiers, time you accepted the terms.
  • Profile: name, age/date of birth, sex, height, weight, calorie and macro goals, preferences.
  • Log: meal photos, nutrition estimates, workouts, water, steps, notes you enter.
  • Device: platform, app version, identifiers needed for sign-in and purchases.
  • Payments: Apple or Google confirm subscription status (via the store and, if used, RevenueCat). We do not store your card number.
  • Apple Health (optional): only if you grant access, we may read or write health/activity data you allow. We do not use HealthKit data for advertising.

3. Why we process data

  • To provide your log, sync, AI photo analysis, goals, and support.
  • To run your account, Premium subscription, and usage limits.
  • To comply with law (including valid authority requests) and protect the service from abuse.

4. GDPR legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (providing the app you request).
  • Consent (for example HealthKit, marketing emails if you opt in, certain analyses).
  • Legitimate interests (security, improving the service, within the law).
  • Legal obligation (accounting, authority requests, where applicable).

5. AI analysis and photos

Meal photos are sent to cloud AI services to estimate foods and nutrients. Do not upload images of other people without a basis, or prohibited content.

We do not sell your photos. We keep them as needed for history and sync until you delete the meal or account, plus a short technical backup window.

6. Who we share with

We do not sell personal data.

Processors: cloud infrastructure and database (Supabase), AI providers for food recognition, Apple/Google for sign-in and payments, RevenueCat for subscription status where used.

Apple and Google receive payment data and, at Sign in, the identity data you choose to share.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers may process data outside the EEA. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (for example Standard Contractual Clauses).

8. Retention

We keep account and log data while you use the service. After account deletion we delete or anonymize it within a reasonable time, except what we must keep by law (for example store transaction evidence).

9. Security

We use encrypted connections, access control, and specialized hosting. No internet transmission is 100% secure.

10. Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If we learn such an account exists, we delete it. Parents can contact contact.veralens@gmail.com.

11. Your rights

You may access, rectify, erase, restrict, object, port data, and withdraw consent. You can delete your account in Profile or email us.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP — dataprotection.ro).

12. Changes

We may update this Policy. The date at the top shows the current version. Continued use after a material change means you have been informed of the new version.

Cancel a Premium subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by VeraLens.

  • iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → CalFoto AI → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
  • Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CalFoto AI → Cancel.

Deleting the app or your CalFoto AI account does not cancel an active store subscription.

Delete your account and data

In the app, open Profile and use the account deletion option. You can also email us and we will delete or anonymize your account data within a reasonable time.
